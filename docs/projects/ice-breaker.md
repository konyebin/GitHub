## What it is

A shared-screen ice breaker for a team of five. Mark who is actually on the call. Spotlight, pairs, and the speaking order use only those people.

## Open it

**[Open the ice breaker](https://konyebin.github.io/GitHub/demos/ice-breaker/index.html)**

## How a call works

1. Type the five names once. They stay in this browser.
2. Uncheck anyone who isn't on the call.
3. **Spotlight** gives each person who showed up one prompt. **Pairs** splits that group into pairs, and folds an odd person into a trio. **One word** is a short lap in a fixed order.
4. If someone joins or drops, toggle their chip. The round updates without starting over.

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in `docs/public/demos/ice-breaker/`. No server and no account.
