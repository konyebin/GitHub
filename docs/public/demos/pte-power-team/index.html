1
Phase 1 of 5

Observe: Foundational Masterclass

Full cohort with alumni demonstrator

Observe experienced sellers navigate challenging customer meetings, supported by facilitator breakdowns.

FocusActive listening, objection recognition, meeting control, and tactical execution.
2
Phase 2 of 5

Practice: Four Core Technology Roleplays

AE/SE pairs

Practice connecting architecture to customer needs across:

FocusThe SE explains the architecture; the AE connects technical capabilities to business outcomes.
Enterprise NetworkingNetwork modernization, cloud versus on-premises management, and operational efficiency.
SecurityZero Trust, security consolidation, threat response, and business resilience.
Data Center & CloudHybrid cloud, AI infrastructure, workload performance, and total cost of ownership.
CollaborationWebex migration, hybrid work, AI productivity, interoperability, and workplace value.
3
Phase 3 of 5

Discover: Consultative Scoping and Customer Discovery

AE/SE pairs

Map customer architecture, explain Cisco Enterprise Agreement value, and conduct discovery with a simulated buying committee.

FocusVisual storytelling, software value, open-ended questioning, pain mapping, MEDDPICC qualification, and coordinated AE/SE execution.
4
Phase 4 of 5

Analyze: Commercial Strategy and RFP Audit

Mixed AE/SE groups of 3–4

Review anonymized Gong recordings, CRM history, and RFP specifications, then present an executive deal defense to an FLM board.

FocusDeal analysis, technical defense, risk mitigation, and commercial alignment.
5
Phase 5 of 5

Demonstrate: Competitive Arena

Three-person debate teams or 4–6-person panels

Navigate competitive displacement, rebuttals, and challenging objections in a debate or multi-vendor panel.

FocusCompetitive differentiation, objection agility, and executive presence.
